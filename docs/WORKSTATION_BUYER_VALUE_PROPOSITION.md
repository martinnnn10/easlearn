# EASLearn Unified Technician Workstation — Buyer Value Proposition

---

## The Problem

Every maintenance manager asks the same question: **"Is this technician ready for independent troubleshooting?"**

Traditional training cannot answer it. Course completion proves attendance, not capability. Quiz scores prove memorization, not methodology. Video libraries prove nothing at all.

When a conveyor goes down at $400/minute, the manager needs to know whether the technician on shift can isolate the fault systematically — or whether they will guess, swap parts, and create a bigger problem.

---

## The Product

**EASLearn's Unified Technician Workstation** is a realistic diagnostic simulation that records exactly how a technician troubleshoots.

The workstation captures:

| What It Records | Why It Matters |
|----------------|---------------|
| Every meter measurement taken | Shows what they tested and in what order |
| Every interpretation recorded | Shows what they concluded from each reading |
| Every hypothesis formed and updated | Shows whether they reason from evidence or guess |
| PLC software state vs. measured field voltage | Shows whether they understand the critical distinction |
| Safety decisions | Shows whether they work safely under pressure |
| Corrective action timing | Shows whether they repair based on confirmed diagnosis or assumption |
| Methodology tier | Shows whether their approach is systematic, guided, exploratory, or random |

## What the Manager Sees

For every technician attempt, the manager can review:

1. **The full diagnostic timeline** — every action in chronological order
2. **Methodology assessment** — systematic, guided, exploratory, or random
3. **Safety status** — clean or violations (with details)
4. **Evidence summary** — which competencies were demonstrated
5. **Readiness recommendation** — ready for validation, needs more practice, or needs safety review

The manager can answer:

- Can this technician troubleshoot systematically?
- Are they guessing?
- Do they understand PLC state versus physical voltage?
- Do they work safely?
- Can they document and explain the repair?
- Are they ready for independent work?
